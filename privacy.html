MemoryBridge Privacy Policy

App: MemoryBridge (Android)
Developer: Hemanth Terli
Effective date: 9 October 2026

MemoryBridge is a personal relationship-memory app. It works with your phone-call recordings to build private notes about the people you talk to (such as promises, events and interests) and shows you a short briefing when someone calls. MemoryBridge is built around one principle: your data stays on your device.

Summary

  • All processing is done on your device using small language models (SLMs).
  • Your recordings, transcripts, contacts and memories are stored only on your device.
  • No data is sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting, and no accounts.

1. Information collected

The developer does not collect any of your information. MemoryBridge does not transmit your data off your device. The app works with the following data that is already on your phone, and only after you grant the relevant permission:

  • Call recordings in the recording folder you choose.
  • Transcripts and memories generated from those recordings on your device.
  • Contacts, to match a phone number to a name.
  • Incoming call number, to look up that person's briefing.
  • Your settings, such as the recording-folder path.
  • Questions you type into the app about a person.

The app does not collect your location, photos or advertising identifiers, and does not ask you to create an account.

2. How information is used

  • To identify who a recording or incoming call is with.
  • To transcribe recordings and extract relationship memories, on your device.
  • To show you a briefing over the incoming-call screen and inside the app.
  • To answer your questions about a person using your stored memories.

Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. Data storage

Everything MemoryBridge creates or uses — transcripts, extracted memories, contact matches and settings — is stored locally on your device. Your original recordings remain in your own recording folder; the app reads them but does not delete them. There is no developer-operated server or cloud storage.

4. Third-party data sharing

We do not share, sell or disclose your data to any third party. Because analysis is performed by SLMs running on your device, your recordings, transcripts and memories are not sent to any external AI service, cloud provider or analytics provider.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because the data lives only on your device, you control it completely.

  • Data is kept on your device until you remove it.
  • Clearing the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → MemoryBridge → Storage) or uninstalling the app deletes the data MemoryBridge stored.
  • You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings.
  • Your original recordings are your own files and are not deleted by the app; delete them with your file manager or recorder app if you wish.

Since the developer holds no copy of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your behalf.

6. Children's privacy

MemoryBridge is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). The developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox and your device's own security (such as screen lock and device encryption). Because nothing is transmitted off the device, your data is not exposed to network interception. We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

Recording or transcribing calls may be subject to consent laws where you or the other party live. You are responsible for complying with them.

8. Permissions and third-party SDKs

Permissions are used only for the on-device features described below; none is used to send data elsewhere.

Android permissionWhy it is used
MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, READ_MEDIA_AUDIOList and read audio files in the call-recording folder you select.
READ_CONTACTSMatch a phone number to a contact name.
READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_CALL_LOGDetect an incoming call and read the caller's number so the briefing can be shown.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOWDisplay the briefing card over the call screen.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USEKeep the briefing popup running while it is displayed.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow the notification required by the foreground service.

The app does not include advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and does not use Firebase.
